Detection settings

Sensor readout appears once data is streaming.

Velocity comes from integrating vertical acceleration. Drift is removed by assuming the bar is at zero velocity whenever it's held still, so reps appear when the bar pauses. Touch-and-go reps with no pause show up together when you rack. If still periods aren't being detected (no reps appearing), raise the still accel/gyro thresholds. MVT is the minimum velocity threshold used for the e1RM estimate.

Device protocol

BLE service b1a7e001-5c3b-4f0e-9a1e-7f2d0b6c0001, notify characteristic b1a7e002-5c3b-4f0e-9a1e-7f2d0b6c0001. Each packet: uint16 seq (index of first sample, wraps), then N samples of int16 ax,ay,az (mg) and int16 gx,gy,gz (0.1 °/s), little-endian, fixed 100 Hz. Dropped samples are filled with the last good sample.
