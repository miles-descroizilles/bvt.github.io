File format

One row per sample at 100 Hz. Lines starting with # are metadata (pd.read_csv(f, comment='#')). Columns: t seconds from start, seq device sample index, ax ay az m/s² and gx gy gz rad/s in the sensor frame (gravity included, nothing filtered), filled = 1 where a dropped sample was replaced with the previous one. The combined CSV adds set_id, lift, load_kg, reps, rpe columns.
